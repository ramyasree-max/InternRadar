# InternRadar
### Discover Opportunities. Build Skills. Shape Your Future.

Finding the right internship can be challenging for students. Opportunities are scattered across different company websites, important updates are easy to miss, and students are often unsure which skills they need to get started.

That's where **InternRadar** comes in.

InternRadar is an AI-powered platform built to make internship hunting easier and help students prepare for their careers. It brings internship opportunities from official company career pages and supported recruitment platforms into one place, so students can spend less time searching and more time developing their skills.

The platform automatically checks its integrated sources every hour to find newly announced internships and track changes to existing opportunities. It also verifies available listings and their original application links to help students make more informed decisions.

But InternRadar isn't just about finding internships. It also helps students understand what companies are looking for. Its AI-powered skill matcher compares a student's skills with internship requirements, highlights missing skills and suggests areas where they can improve.

Students can also keep track of their applications, set personal learning goals, monitor their progress and stay updated with the latest developments in the technology and startup industries.

### Key Features

- **Genuine Internship Opportunities:** Discover internships collected from official company websites and supported recruitment platforms.
- **Hourly Updates:** Automatically monitor integrated sources every 60 minutes for newly published and updated opportunities.
- **AI Skill Matcher:** Understand how your current skills compare with the requirements of an internship.
- **Personalised Recommendations:** Find opportunities that match your skills, education and interests.
- **Application Tracker:** Keep all your internship applications organised and follow their progress.
- **Career Growth Centre:** Set learning goals, build consistent habits and track your personal development.
- **Industry News:** Keep up with the latest updates in AI, software development, cybersecurity, cloud computing and startups.
- **Company Directory:** Explore companies, their official websites and connected recruitment sources.
- **Source Transparency:** See where internship listings come from, when sources were last checked and which opportunities are currently verified.
- **Smart Notifications:** Stay informed about new opportunities, important listing changes and upcoming deadlines.

### Technology Stack

React.js · Vite · JavaScript · Tailwind CSS · Node.js · Express.js · MongoDB · Google Gemini API
